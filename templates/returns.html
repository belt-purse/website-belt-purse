Customer Care

Exchange Policy

We offer exchange only in rare cases. If you receive a product with an issue, you can raise an exchange request within 7 days of delivery.

Conditions

  • Item must be unused
  • Product should be in its original packaging
  • Product tag must not be removed from the product
  • Exchange will be considered only after the issue is reviewed by our team

How to Request an Exchange

To request an exchange, please go to:

Login / Account > Help Center > Submit an Issue

Our team will review your request and process it accordingly.

You can also contact us through

Email: beltpurse.com@gmail.com

WhatsApp: +91 7000747936

Processing

Once your request is verified, we will proceed with the exchange process.
