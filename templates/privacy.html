Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. At Belt Purse Store, we respect your personal information and are committed to protecting the details you share with us while using our website, placing an order, making an online payment, creating an account, or contacting our support team.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and how your data may be handled while shopping on our website.

Information We Collect

When you place an order, create an account, or use our website, we may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Shipping address
  • Billing/order details
  • Order history
  • Payment status
  • Support or issue details submitted through the Help Center

We collect only the information required to process your order, provide customer support, maintain your account, and improve your shopping experience.

How We Use Your Information

  • To process and confirm your orders
  • To deliver your products safely
  • To share order updates, payment updates, and delivery details
  • To maintain your account and order history
  • To respond to your support requests or issues
  • To improve our website, products, and customer experience
  • To prevent fraud, misuse, or unauthorized activity
  • To communicate important updates related to your order or account

Your personal information is used only for order processing, service improvement, customer support, and business-related communication.

Online Payments

Belt Purse Store accepts online payments only. We do not offer Cash on Delivery.

Payments on our website are processed through Razorpay, a secure third-party payment gateway. We do not store your complete card details, UPI details, net banking details, or any sensitive banking information on our website.

Payment information is handled securely by Razorpay as per their own privacy and security standards.

Google Analytics and Website Usage

We use Google Analytics to understand website engagement, visitor activity, page performance, and customer browsing behavior. This helps us improve our website experience and understand which products, pages, or sections are useful for our customers.

Google Analytics may collect technical information such as device type, browser type, pages visited, time spent on the website, and general usage activity. This information is used for analysis and website improvement only.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, remember cart activity, understand website performance, and make the website easier to use.

You can disable cookies from your browser settings, but some features of the website may not work properly after disabling them.

Marketing Communication

Currently, we may not send regular promotional messages or newsletters. However, in the future, we may use your email or phone number to share product updates, offers, new arrivals, or other marketing communication.

You can contact us anytime if you do not wish to receive promotional updates from Belt Purse Store.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep your personal information safe and protected. Your details are handled carefully and are not shared publicly.

However, no online platform can guarantee complete security. By using our website, you understand that data transmission over the internet may involve some level of risk.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

Your information may be shared only when necessary with trusted service providers, such as:

  • Razorpay for online payment processing
  • Delivery or courier partners for order shipment
  • Website hosting or technical service providers
  • Customer support or order management tools
  • Analytics tools such as Google Analytics

This sharing is done only to complete your order, process your payment, deliver your product, provide support, or improve our services.

Account and Order History

When you create an account or place an order, your order history and basic account details may be saved on our website. This helps you view your past orders, track purchases, and receive better support in case of any issue.

Help Center and Support Requests

If you submit an issue through the Help Center, we may collect the details you provide, including your order information, issue description, images, or contact details. This information is used only to review your request and provide support.

Your Rights

You can contact us to request correction or deletion of your personal information, subject to order records, legal requirements, payment records, or business record requirements.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, payment system, analytics setup, services, or business practices. The updated policy will be posted on this page.
